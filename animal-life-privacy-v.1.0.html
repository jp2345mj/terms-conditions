Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0

Last Updated: November 2026

App: Animal Life: Dress Up & Play

In short: Animal Life is a game for children and it collects nothing about them. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Everything your child does in the game stays on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how "Animal Life: Dress Up & Play" (the "App"), developed by Laae Collective LLC ("we", "our", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on the Apple App Store and on Google Play. The App is made for children and their families, and we designed it so that we do not need any personal information to run it.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. In particular:

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no email address is asked for.
  • We do not collect names, ages, photos, voice, location, contacts or device identifiers.
  • The App has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not include third-party advertising or data-collection software.
  • The App does not use the camera, the microphone, photos, contacts or location.
  • We do not know who plays the App or what they do in it.

Names a child types for their animal and its friends, and everything else they make or collect (animal friends, houses, stars, items, progress and settings), are saved only on the device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the App deletes this saved data.

3. How the App Works Without Data

The App works offline. It does not need an internet connection to play. It only connects to the internet when a grown-up chooses to buy something in the App's Store or to restore earlier purchases, or opens one of the web links described below.

4. Purchases

The App is free to download. A grown-up can buy extra worlds to explore and places (such as an arcade, a shop or a restaurant) in the App's Store. All payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see or store card or payment details. The store tells the App only that a purchase was made so it can be unlocked on your devices. Every purchase, "Restore purchases" and each link to our Terms and Privacy Policy are behind a grown-up check, which is asked again each time, so children cannot make a purchase or leave the App by accident. Stars earned by playing are a play item and cannot be bought with real money.

5. Links to the Web

The only places the App opens the internet are the links to our Terms and Conditions and this Privacy Policy, after the grown-up check. They open in your browser, which has its own privacy practices.

6. Information Sharing and Disclosure

Because we do not collect personal information, we have nothing to sell, trade or share. We would only disclose information if the law required it, and we have none about your child to give.

7. Data Storage and Security

Game data is stored in the App's own storage on your device, protected by the security of your device. If you back up your device, the backup may include the App's saved game under your own backup settings. Please keep your device protected with a passcode.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We hold no data about you or your child, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. To erase the game's saved data from a device, delete the App.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for children and follows the rules for apps made for children, including those of the Apple Kids Category and Google Play's Families policies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the App does not ask for it. Parents and guardians who have questions can contact us at any time (see below).

10. Your Rights and Choices

Since no personal information is collected, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You are always free to contact us with questions about privacy.

11. Data Minimization

  • We collect nothing that is not needed to run the game, and the game does not need personal information.
  • Saved game data stays on the device.
  • If a future update ever added a feature that used information, we would update this policy first and ask for a parent's permission where the law requires it.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is available through the links in the App and in our Terms and Conditions. Changes become effective when posted.
